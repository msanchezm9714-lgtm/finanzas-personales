The whisper-tiny files in this directory are the quantized ONNX conversion from
https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/whisper-tiny of OpenAI Whisper Tiny.
The underlying model is licensed under Apache-2.0:
https://huggingface.co/openai/whisper-tiny

The runtime files are distributed with @huggingface/transformers 3.8.1.
They are served by this application so voice recordings can be transcribed
in the browser without uploading them to a third-party inference service.
